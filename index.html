Quality Assurance

Testing & Verification Protocol

Industrial containment demands zero margin for error. We subject 100% of our fabricated vessels to strict third-party and in-house inspection standards.

100% Radiography Testing (RT)

Full gamma X-Ray examination of longitudinal and circumferential weld seams per ASME Section VIII Div 1 UW-51 to detect any porosity, slag, or lack of fusion.

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing

Vessels are filled with treated water and tested at 1.5× the maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP) with calibrated digital gauges for a minimum 4-hour hold period.

Automated Submerged Arc (SAW)

Continuous automatic SAW column & boom welding ensures deep, consistent weld penetration, superior mechanical tensile properties, and smooth bead aesthetics.

SA 2.5 Blasting & Epoxy Coating

Grit blasting to Swedish standard SA 2.5 near-white metal finish followed by high-build anti-corrosive coal tar epoxy (underground) or 350-micron aliphatic polyurethane.
